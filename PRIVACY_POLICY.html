Privacy Policy & Help — Vidan Pulley Generator

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Developer: Vidan

1. Information We Collect

Vidan Pulley Generator collects only the minimum information strictly required to verify your software license and prevent unauthorized redistribution:

  • Autodesk App Store Edition: Your Autodesk User ID (userId) provided by Autodesk Fusion, which is sent to the official Autodesk Entitlement API to confirm your purchase/entitlement.
  • Direct Sale Edition (Lemon Squeezy): Your license key and a one-way SHA-256 hardware fingerprint hash of your workstation, used to bind activation to your permitted number of devices.

We do not collect CAD models, geometry files, personal emails, browsing history, or telemetry.

2. Third-Party Sharing

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Verification requests are transmitted solely to Autodesk (apps.exchange.autodesk.com) or Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com) depending on your edition.

3. Data Retention

Local activation data (~/.vidan_pulley_license.dat) is stored on your computer only for as long as the add-in is installed and activated. Server-side entitlement/license records are retained according to Autodesk or Lemon Squeezy retention policies while your license remains active.

4. Data Deletion & Support

You may remove local license data at any time by deleting ~/.vidan_pulley_license.dat from your home directory. To request deletion of server-side activation records or for technical support, contact: support@vidan-eu3d.example.
